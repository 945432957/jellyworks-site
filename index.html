Privacy Policy

Too Many Jellies is a single-player game that runs entirely on your device. We do not collect, store or share any personal data.

What the game stores

Your progress (levels, upgrades, settings) and whether you bought the Full Game are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data. Nothing is sent to us.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

The game has no sign-in, shows no ads, contains no analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Purchases

The Full Game unlock is sold through Apple's App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to that process.

Children

Because we collect no data from anyone, we collect no data from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page. Questions: lipeink888@gmail.com.

隐私政策

《果冻防线》是一款完全在你设备上运行的单机游戏。我们不收集、不保存、也不分享任何个人信息。

游戏保存了什么

游戏进度（关卡、升级、设置）和是否购买了完整版，只保存在你的设备上。删除 App 时这些数据会一起删除，不会发送给我们。

没有账号、广告、统计和追踪

游戏不需要登录，没有广告，不含任何统计或广告 SDK，也不会跨 App 或网站追踪你。

购买

完整版通过 Apple 的 App Store 购买，付款全部由 Apple 处理，我们看不到你的付款信息。这部分适用 Apple 的隐私政策。

儿童

我们不收集任何人的数据，因此也不收集儿童的数据。

变更与联系

本政策如有变化，会更新在本页面。有问题请发邮件到 lipeink888@gmail.com。

Support / 技术支持

Email lipeink888@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days. 发邮件到上面的地址，一般 2 个工作日内回复。

I bought the Full Game but chapters 3–6 are locked / 买了完整版但第 3–6 章还是锁着

Open Settings (gear on the home screen) and tap Restore under Full Game, using the same Apple Account you bought it with.
在首页点齿轮打开设置，在「完整版」一行点「恢复购买」（用购买时的 Apple 账户）。

The game runs slowly / 游戏卡顿

The game lowers effects automatically on busy screens. Closing other apps and keeping the phone cool helps.
场面很大时游戏会自动降低特效。关掉其他 App、让手机别太烫会有帮助。

Lost progress after reinstalling / 重装后进度没了

Progress is stored on the device only, so deleting the app removes it. Your Full Game purchase can always be restored.
进度只存在本机，删除 App 会一起删除；完整版的购买随时可以恢复。
